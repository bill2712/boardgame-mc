How to host a great game night with a phone moderator

First time running Werewolf, Avalon or Cheese Thief for friends? How to prepare, how to use voice narration, digital role cards and timers, and what to do after the game.

Before you start: five minutes of prep

  • Pick a game for your group size. The home page filters by player count, and every game lists its best count. For a first game, choose something short and simple such as Cheese Thief or One Night Ultimate Werewolf.
  • Sit in a circle and number yourselves clockwise, starting next to the phone. Every "player 3" in the narration means this seat number.
  • Type player names on the setup page so the narration calls people by name. Beginners follow much more easily.
  • Tap ⚙ Settings › Test voice to make sure you can hear it. On iPhone, switch off the silent switch and turn the volume up.
  • Put the phone in the middle of the table, or connect a Bluetooth speaker, so everyone can hear.

Dealing roles without cards

With digital dealing on, the phone is passed around. When it is your turn, tap to view your role, memorise it, tap Hide and pass it on. Everyone else turns away or closes their eyes.

Someone forgot their role? Tap 🪪 at the top right during the game, pick the seat and hold the button to see the card again; it hides when you let go. The narration pauses automatically.

During the game: let the phone moderate

  • Night phases are automatic: the voice calls each role to wake, act and sleep, with enough time to act. If it feels too fast or slow, change the night pace in Settings.
  • Three buttons at the bottom: ↺ repeat the last line, ⏸ pause, ⏭ skip a wait. Pause when someone steps away.
  • Rules question? Tap 📖 at the top right to read the rules and role tips without leaving the game.
  • The discussion timer warns at one minute and ten seconds left and chimes at zero, so rounds do not drag on.

After the game: stats and another round

  • The winner is recorded automatically at the end; if the phone cannot tell, it asks you to tap who won. Each game's stats appear on its setup page.
  • Play again reshuffles the roles and keeps the same players and settings.
  • Afterwards, reveal everyone's roles and talk through what happened. This is often the best part.

Tips for playing with beginners

  • Use as few special roles as possible in the first game, then add more once people are comfortable.
  • Explain only the win conditions before you start; details can be learned while playing.
  • Remind everyone that lying is part of the game. No hard feelings afterwards!
