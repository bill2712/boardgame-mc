How to choose

  • Lots of beginners: pick simple word or party games such as Undercover, Just One or Charades.
  • Love mind games: pick team deduction such as Avalon, The Resistance or Secret Hitler.
  • Short on time: One Night Ultimate Werewolf, Cheese Thief and Chameleon finish in about fifteen minutes.
  • Nobody wants to sit out: pick co-op or team games such as Wavelength or Fishbowl.
