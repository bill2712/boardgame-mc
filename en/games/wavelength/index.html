Wavelength: moderator & rules guide

One clue from the psychic; can your team find the exact spot?

👥 2–20 players⏳ 30 min⭐ Best 4–10Party games
▶ Start with the app

Want to play Wavelength without anyone sitting out as moderator? Board Game MC narrates it for you in Cantonese, Mandarin or English, with timers and optional digital role dealing. Also known as 心靈同步, 心灵同步, 同步率.

📖 How to play

  1. Two teams take turns. Each turn, one psychic from the team holds the phone.
  2. A spectrum card appears, such as "Cold ↔ Hot". Only the psychic sees the target, by holding a button.
  3. The psychic gives one clue (e.g. "hot pot") and the team turns the needle to where they think it goes.
  4. Reveal: bullseye 4 points, close 3, near 2. The first team to the target score wins.

💡 Tips

  • As psychic, think about how your team sees it, not just you.
  • For a target near the middle, pick an example everyone agrees is "average".
  • Ask "is this extreme or just a bit?" to place the needle.

❓ FAQ

Do I need the physical game?

No. The phone deals spectrum cards, shows the dial and keeps score.
