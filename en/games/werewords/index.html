Werewords: moderator & rules guide

Twenty Questions meets Werewolf

👥 4–10 players⏳ 10 min⭐ Best 5–8Word games
▶ Start with the app

Want to play Werewords without anyone sitting out as moderator? Board Game MC narrates it for you in Cantonese, Mandarin or English, with timers and optional digital role dealing. Also known as 狼人真言, 狼人問答.

📖 How to play

  1. The Mayor (public) knows the magic word and answers yes/no questions with Yes, No, Maybe or So Close.
  2. The Werewolf and the Seer also know the word. The Werewolf wants nobody to guess it; the Seer wants to help without being found.
  3. If the word is guessed in time, the werewolves may name one player: if it is the Seer, the werewolves win; otherwise the village wins.
  4. If nobody guesses it, everyone votes for a werewolf. Catch one and the village wins; otherwise the werewolves win.

💡 Tips

  • The Seer should ask helpful questions without being too obvious.
  • Werewolves can ask misleading questions to waste time.
  • When the Mayor says "So close", chase that direction fast.

❓ FAQ

How is Werewords different from Werewolf?

Werewords is a word guessing game with hidden roles. One round takes about ten minutes, with no elimination.
