Charades: moderator & rules guide

Team guessing race: act, describe or draw

👥 2–30 players⏳ 15 min⭐ Best 6+Party games
▶ Start with the app

Want to play Charades without anyone sitting out as moderator? Board Game MC narrates it for you in Cantonese, Mandarin or English, with timers and optional digital role dealing. Also known as 比手畫腳, 比手划脚, 估字遊戲.

📖 How to play

  1. Split into two or more teams. Each turn, one performer from a team holds the phone while teammates guess.
  2. The performer gives hints in the chosen mode: act it out, describe it, or draw it.
  3. Tap ✅ for a correct guess or ⏭ to skip a hard one. When time runs out, the next team plays.
  4. After all rounds, the team with the most points wins.

💡 Tips

  • Signal the category first, like animal or action.
  • Skip hard ones fast; quantity beats difficulty.
  • When describing, use opposites or related things.

❓ FAQ

What modes are there?

Three: act it out, describe it, or draw it. Pick the category and word language too.
