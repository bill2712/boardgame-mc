Werewolf: moderator & rules guide

Fully automatic moderator: deals roles, runs the night, announces deaths

👥 6–16 players⏳ 30–60 min⭐ Best 9–12Social deduction

Want to play Werewolf without anyone sitting out as moderator? Board Game MC narrates it for you in Cantonese, Mandarin or English, with timers and optional digital role dealing. Also known as 狼人殺, 狼人杀, 天黑請閉眼.

📖 How to play

  1. No moderator needed. The phone deals roles, and at night each role takes a turn choosing on the phone (keep it in the middle of the table).
  2. Night order: Guard → Werewolves → Witch → Seer. At dawn the phone announces who died.
  3. During the day players speak in seat order, then vote to exile one player.
  4. Good wins when every werewolf is gone. Werewolves win by killing all special roles or all villagers ("side"), or every good player ("all").
  5. Guarded and healed on the same night still dies. Poison ignores the Guard. A poisoned Hunter cannot shoot.

🎭 Roles

RoleTeamAbility
🐺 WerewolfEvilEach night, choose a victim with the other werewolves.
🔮 SeerGoodEach night, check whether a player is a werewolf.
🧪 WitchGoodOne healing potion and one poison, each usable once; at most one per night.
🏹 HunterGoodWhen you die you may shoot a player (not if poisoned).
🛡️ GuardGoodEach night protect a player from the wolves; not the same player two nights in a row.
🤪 IdiotGoodIf voted out, reveal and survive, but lose your vote.
👨‍🌾 VillagerGoodNo special ability. Find the werewolves by reasoning.

💡 Tips

  • The Seer can claim on day one or stay hidden until the key moment.
  • Witches usually heal on night one, when there is the least information.
  • The Guard cannot protect the same player two nights in a row anyway, so vary it.
  • Werewolves should reason like villagers; going quiet looks suspicious.

❓ FAQ

Can you play Werewolf without a moderator?

Yes. The app is a fully automatic moderator: it deals roles, takes night actions on the phone, announces deaths and detects the winner.

What is "side" vs "all" win condition?

"Side": werewolves win by killing all special roles or all villagers. "All": they must kill every good player. "Side" is faster.

What if the Guard and the Witch protect the same player?

By common rules, a player who is both guarded and healed on the same night still dies.
