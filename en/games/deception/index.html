Deception: Murder in Hong Kong: moderator & rules guide

A Hong Kong murder: the Forensic Scientist can only hint

👥 4–12 players⏳ 20 min⭐ Best 6–10Social deduction
▶ Start with the app

Want to play Deception: Murder in Hong Kong without anyone sitting out as moderator? Board Game MC narrates it for you in Cantonese, Mandarin or English, with timers and optional digital role dealing. Also known as 詭影危機, 诡影危机, Deception Murder in Hong Kong.

📖 How to play

  1. The Forensic Scientist (public) knows the truth but cannot speak, only hint with bullet markers on scene tiles.
  2. Each investigator has 4 Means cards and 4 Clue cards. The murderer is one of them and picks the real means and key evidence.
  3. Three rounds: each round the Forensic Scientist places or swaps a scene tile, then everyone presents their theory.
  4. Each player has one badge to guess "means + key evidence". A correct guess wins for the investigators; after three rounds the murderer wins.
  5. With 6+ players add the Accomplice and Witness: after a correct solve, the murderer team still wins if they identify the Witness.

💡 Tips

  • As Forensic Scientist, start with broad markers and narrow down.
  • The murderer should champion a plausible but wrong combination.
  • The Witness knows the culprits, but accusing too early gets them killed.

❓ FAQ

How does the Forensic Scientist play?

The Forensic Scientist knows the answer but cannot speak, hinting only with bullet markers. The app narrates the night and times the three rounds.
