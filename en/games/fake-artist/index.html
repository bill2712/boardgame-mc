A Fake Artist Goes to New York: moderator & rules guide

Everyone adds one stroke; the fake has no idea what it is

👥 5–10 players⏳ 10 min⭐ Best 6–8Party games

Want to play A Fake Artist Goes to New York without anyone sitting out as moderator? Board Game MC narrates it for you in Cantonese, Mandarin or English, with timers and optional digital role dealing. Also known as 假藝術家, 假艺术家, 紐約假畫家.

📖 How to play

  1. The phone secretly deals the prompt: everyone sees the topic and the word, the fake artist only sees the topic.
  2. On one shared sheet, each player adds one continuous stroke, for two rounds.
  3. Then everyone points at the fake artist at once.
  4. If the fake is caught, they may still guess the word and win. If the wrong player is caught, the fake wins.

💡 Tips

  • A first stroke that is too clear gives the answer away.
  • The fake can draw ambiguous lines that fit anything.
  • Watch for strokes that only follow what others drew.

❓ FAQ

What do I need?

One sheet of paper and a different colour pen per player. The phone deals the prompt, so nobody has to be question master.
