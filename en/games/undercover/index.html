Who's the Undercover: moderator & rules guide

Everyone gets a word, but one is slightly different

👥 4–12 players⏳ 15 min⭐ Best 6–10Word games
▶ Start with the app

Want to play Who's the Undercover without anyone sitting out as moderator? Board Game MC narrates it for you in Cantonese, Mandarin or English, with timers and optional digital role dealing. Also known as 誰是臥底, 谁是卧底, 臥底遊戲.

📖 How to play

  1. The phone deals words: civilians share one word, the undercover gets a similar one, and a blank gets none.
  2. Each round, everyone describes their word in one sentence without saying it.
  3. After each round, vote. The player with the most votes is out.
  4. Civilians win when every undercover (and blank) is out. Undercovers win when they equal or outnumber the civilians, or only two players remain.

💡 Tips

  • Keep round one vague and listen first; you might be the one with the odd word.
  • Once you realise your word differs, shift your descriptions to match the group.
  • A blank should give broad, safe clues like "you see it every day".
  • When voting, look for descriptions that are slightly off.

❓ FAQ

How do you play Who's the Undercover?

Civilians share a word and the undercover gets a similar one. Each round everyone describes theirs, then votes someone out. Find all undercovers to win.

Can we play with English words?

Yes. Choose Chinese, English or both in the options.
