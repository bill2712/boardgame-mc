Codenames: moderator & rules guide

Two spymasters, one-word clues, find your agents

👥 4–20 players⏳ 15 min⭐ Best 4–8Word games
▶ Start with the app

Want to play Codenames without anyone sitting out as moderator? Board Game MC narrates it for you in Cantonese, Mandarin or English, with timers and optional digital role dealing. Also known as 機密代號, 机密代号, 行動代號.

📖 How to play

  1. Split into Red and Blue, each with a spymaster. There are 25 words; only the spymasters see the key card showing which words are their agents.
  2. On your turn the spymaster gives one word and one number, such as "Fruit, 2".
  3. Teammates then tap words: their own colour lets them keep guessing (up to the number + 1); neutral or the other colour ends the turn.
  4. The first team to find all its agents wins. Touching the assassin loses instantly.
  5. Play fully on the phone (word board plus hold-to-view key card), or use it only as a key card and timer with the physical cards.

💡 Tips

  • A great clue links two or three words while steering clear of the assassin.
  • Spymasters must not hint with faces or gestures.
  • When unsure, end the turn rather than risk the assassin.

❓ FAQ

Can we play without the Codenames box?

Yes. Choose "Digital word board": the phone deals 25 words and spymasters hold a button to see the key.
