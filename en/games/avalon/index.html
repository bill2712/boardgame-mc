The Resistance: Avalon: moderator & rules guide

Arthurian loyalty vs. Mordred's hidden minions

👥 5–10 players⏳ 30 min⭐ Best 7–10Social deduction
▶ Start with the app

Want to play The Resistance: Avalon without anyone sitting out as moderator? Board Game MC narrates it for you in Cantonese, Mandarin or English, with timers and optional digital role dealing. Also known as 阿瓦隆, 抵抗組織：阿瓦隆, 抵抗组织：阿瓦隆.

📖 How to play

  1. Good wins by completing 3 quests. Evil wins by failing 3 quests, or when 5 team proposals in a row are rejected.
  2. Each round the leader proposes a team, everyone votes openly, and the team then secretly plays Success / Fail cards.
  3. With 7+ players, quest 4 needs two Fail cards to fail.
  4. If good completes 3 quests, the Assassin gets one guess at Merlin. A hit means evil wins.
  5. The app narrates the night and tracks quests, votes and the assassination.

🧭 The Resistance: Avalon role strategy

🧙

Merlin

  • Guide with votes and subtle hints; avoid naming evil players outright.
  • Make the odd small mistake on purpose to confuse the Assassin.
🛡️

Percival

  • Work out which of the two thumbs is the real Merlin and protect them.
  • Act like Merlin to draw the Assassin's attention.
⚔️

Loyal Servant of Arthur

  • Use quest results and voting records to find evil.
  • Do not blindly trust anyone claiming to be Merlin.
🗡️

Assassin

  • Watch who is "too accurate" all game; that is probably Merlin.
  • Compare notes with your team before the final strike.
🔮

Morgana

  • Act like Merlin in front of Percival.
  • Support good teams at times to build trust.
👑

Mordred

  • Merlin cannot see you, so play the good guy boldly.
  • You are the hardest evil to spot; get onto teams.
🌑

Oberon

  • You do not know your team; deduce them from results.
  • Fail when useful, but beware of exposing yourself alongside a teammate.
😈

Minion of Mordred

  • Coordinate votes, but do not always reject together.
  • Get onto teams so you can play Fail.

💡 Tips

  • Merlin should guide the good team subtly; being obvious gets you assassinated.
  • As Percival, protect Merlin by drawing attention so the Assassin suspects you.
  • Watch who keeps approving teams that end up failing.
  • Quest 1 gives the least information, so good players can propose freely and learn from the result.

❓ FAQ

What player count is best for Avalon?

5 to 10 players. It shines at 7 to 10, where you can add Morgana, Mordred and Oberon.

How does Avalon's night phase go?

Evil players see each other, evil thumbs up for Merlin (except Mordred), then Merlin and Morgana thumbs up for Percival. The app reads the right script for your roles.

Why does quest 4 need two fails?

With 7 or more players, quest 4 only fails with two Fail cards. The quest tracker reminds you.
