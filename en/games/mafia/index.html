Mafia: moderator & rules guide

The classic "close your eyes" game with an automatic moderator

👥 5–16 players⏳ 20–40 min⭐ Best 8–12Social deduction
▶ Start with the app

Want to play Mafia without anyone sitting out as moderator? Board Game MC narrates it for you in Cantonese, Mandarin or English, with timers and optional digital role dealing. Also known as 殺手遊戲, 杀手游戏, 天黑請閉眼.

📖 How to play

  1. No moderator needed. The phone deals roles, and at night the Mafia, Doctor and Detective take turns on the phone (keep it in the middle).
  2. Night order: Mafia → Doctor → Detective. At dawn the phone announces who died.
  3. During the day everyone discusses, then votes to eliminate one player.
  4. The town wins when every Mafia member is gone. The Mafia win once they equal or outnumber everyone else alive.

🎭 Roles

RoleTeamAbility
🔪 MafiaEvilEach night, choose a victim with your partners.
👮 DetectiveGoodEach night, check whether a player is Mafia.
🩺 DoctorGoodEach night, protect one player from the Mafia.
🙂 CivilianGoodFind the Mafia by talking and voting.

🧭 Mafia role strategy

🔪

Mafia

  • Target the Detective or Doctor first.
  • Go along with the crowd during the day to stay out of focus.
👮

Detective

  • When you find the Mafia, hint first and reveal when the time is right.
  • Have enough evidence before you claim.
🩺

Doctor

  • Protect the most influential town player.
  • Never reveal that you are the Doctor.
🙂

Civilian

  • Watch who always follows or suddenly switches votes.
  • Voice your suspicions; silence only helps the Mafia.

💡 Tips

  • The Detective does not have to reveal a find at once; hint first to avoid being killed that night.
  • The Doctor should guess who the Mafia will target, usually the most influential speaker.
  • The Mafia should join the discussion; staying quiet looks suspicious.

❓ FAQ

How is Mafia different from Werewolf?

Mafia is the classic, simpler version with just Mafia, Detective, Doctor and civilians. Werewolf adds roles like the Witch, Hunter and Guard.

Can we play without a moderator?

Yes. The phone deals roles, runs the night, tracks deaths and detects the winner.
