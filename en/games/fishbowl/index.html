Fishbowl: moderator & rules guide

Same words, three rounds: describe → one word → act

👥 4–30 players⏳ 30 min⭐ Best 6+Party games
▶ Start with the app

Want to play Fishbowl without anyone sitting out as moderator? Board Game MC narrates it for you in Cantonese, Mandarin or English, with timers and optional digital role dealing. Also known as 沙律碗, 沙拉碗, 魚缸遊戲.

📖 How to play

  1. Split into two teams. The phone fills a bowl with words (you can add your own too).
  2. Round 1: describe in words. Round 2: one word only. Round 3: act it out. All rounds use the same words.
  3. Each turn one performer holds the phone. Tap ✅ for a correct guess; skipped words go back in the bowl. When time is up, the other team plays.
  4. When the bowl is empty, the next round starts. After three rounds, the team with the most points wins.

💡 Tips

  • Remember the clues from round 1; they make rounds 2 and 3 much faster.
  • In round 2, use the key word everyone remembers from round 1.
  • Skip hard words and leave them for the other team.

❓ FAQ

Can we use our own words?

Yes. Add words in Settings › Custom words, then choose "Built-in + custom" or "Custom only" in the game options.
