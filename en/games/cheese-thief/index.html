Cheese Thief: moderator & rules guide

Roll to see when you wake. Who stole the cheese?

👥 4–8 players⏳ 10 min⭐ Best 6–8Social deduction
▶ Start with the app

Want to play Cheese Thief without anyone sitting out as moderator? Board Game MC narrates it for you in Cantonese, Mandarin or English, with timers and optional digital role dealing. Also known as 奶酪大盜, 奶酪大盗, 乳酪大盜.

📖 How to play

  1. Everyone rolls a die (two dice with 4 players) and peeks: that number is the hour you wake up (1 to 6).
  2. If you are the only one awake at your hour, you may peek at one other player's die (not in 4-player games).
  3. The Cheese Thief must steal the cheese when they wake, even if others are watching.
  4. At dawn, discuss and then everyone points at one player at once. If the thief is among the most-voted, the Sleepyheads win; otherwise the thief and Followers win.
  5. Fall Mouse (optional, 6–8 players): wins alone by getting the most votes.

🎭 Roles

RoleTeamAbility
🧀 Cheese ThiefEvilWhen you wake you must steal the cheese. Avoid getting the most votes.
😴 SleepyheadGoodIf you wake alone you may peek at one other die. Vote out the Cheese Thief to win.
🙈 Fall MouseNeutralActs like a Sleepyhead, but you win alone if you get the most votes.

🧭 Cheese Thief role strategy

🧀

Cheese Thief

  • Claim you woke alone at another hour and peeked at a die.
  • If someone saw you steal, accuse them first in the morning.
😴

Sleepyhead

  • When alone, peek at the most suspicious player's die.
  • Vouch for whoever woke with you.
🙈

Fall Mouse

  • Say slightly contradictory things to attract votes.
  • Do not overdo it, or everyone will guess you are the Fall Mouse.

👥 Recommended setups

PlayersSetup
44 players: no Followers; everyone rolls two dice and nobody may peek.
55 players: a Sleepyhead awake when the thief steals becomes a Follower (if several, the thief points at one).
66 players: before dawn the thief picks 1 Follower; they see each other.
77 players: the thief picks 2 Followers who see each other but not the thief.
88 players: the thief picks 2 Followers, and all three see each other.

💡 Tips

  • When you wake alone, peek at the die of the player you trust least to check their story.
  • Whoever woke with you is your best witness; vouch for each other in the morning.
  • As the thief, claim you woke alone and peeked at someone to cause confusion.
  • Followers don't know the thief's number, so listen to everyone's timeline before you lie.

❓ FAQ

Can you play Cheese Thief without a moderator?

Yes. The app calls hours 1 to 6 with voice narration and runs the Follower step for your player count, so everyone can play.

What is the minimum number of players?

4 to 8. With 4 players everyone rolls two dice and there are no Followers. It plays best with 6 to 8.

What if I have no dice or cards?

Turn on "Digital roles and dice". The phone deals roles and dice, and lone wakers can peek on the phone.
