Just One: moderator & rules guide

Co-op guessing: everyone writes one clue, duplicates get erased

👥 3–12 players⏳ 20 min⭐ Best 5–7Word games
▶ Start with the app

Want to play Just One without anyone sitting out as moderator? Board Game MC narrates it for you in Cantonese, Mandarin or English, with timers and optional digital role dealing. Also known as 超級犯規, 一詞提示, 只有一個.

📖 How to play

  1. Everyone plays together. Each round one player is the guesser and closes their eyes; everyone else sees the mystery word on the phone.
  2. Each player secretly writes one clue word on paper.
  3. Compare clues: identical or too-similar clues are all removed, leaving only unique ones.
  4. The guesser opens their eyes, reads the remaining clues and guesses once: correct scores 1; passing skips the card; a wrong guess loses an extra card. 13 cards in total.

💡 Tips

  • Avoid the most obvious clue; someone else will probably write it too and both get removed.
  • Give clues from different angles: colour, place, use, sound.
  • If unsure, passing is better than a wrong guess, which loses an extra card.

❓ FAQ

What is the best player count?

3 to 7 players. More players means more duplicate clues and a bigger challenge.
