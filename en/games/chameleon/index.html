The Chameleon: moderator & rules guide

Give one clue word. The Chameleon must blend in

👥 3–8 players⏳ 15 min⭐ Best 5–7Word games
▶ Start with the app

Want to play The Chameleon without anyone sitting out as moderator? Board Game MC narrates it for you in Cantonese, Mandarin or English, with timers and optional digital role dealing. Also known as 變色龍, 变色龙, Chameleon.

📖 How to play

  1. A topic card with 16 words is shown to everyone. The phone secretly tells everyone which word is the answer, except the Chameleon.
  2. Starting with one player, each person says one word related to the answer: too obvious and the Chameleon will guess it, too vague and you look suspicious.
  3. After a short discussion, everyone points at one player.
  4. If the Chameleon is caught, they get one guess at the answer. A correct guess means they escape! If the wrong player is caught, the Chameleon wins.

💡 Tips

  • Give clues teammates understand but that do not pin down the word.
  • Going later helps the Chameleon borrow from earlier clues.
  • Too vague looks suspicious; too obvious gives it away.

❓ FAQ

How many players?

3 to 8 players, best with 5 to 7.
