The Resistance: moderator & rules guide

The original of Avalon: no special roles, pure deduction

👥 5–10 players⏳ 30 min⭐ Best 6–8Social deduction
▶ Start with the app

Want to play The Resistance without anyone sitting out as moderator? Board Game MC narrates it for you in Cantonese, Mandarin or English, with timers and optional digital role dealing. Also known as 抵抗組織, 抵抗组织, Resistance.

📖 How to play

  1. The Resistance wins with 3 successful missions. Spies win with 3 failed missions, or when 5 team proposals in a row are rejected.
  2. Each round the leader picks a team and everyone votes openly; the team then secretly plays Success or Fail.
  3. With 7+ players, mission 4 needs two Fail cards to fail.
  4. At night only the spies open their eyes to see each other. No special roles, so it is great for beginners.

🧭 The Resistance role strategy

✊

Resistance member

  • Record who went on each mission and the result, then cross-check.
  • Do not keep sending the same suspicious group.
🕶️

Spy

  • Do not fail every mission; let one pass to build trust.
  • Split your votes from your fellow spies to avoid suspicion.

💡 Tips

  • Mission 1 gives the least information, so propose freely and learn from the result.
  • Track every vote: whoever keeps approving failed teams is suspicious.
  • Spies do not have to fail every mission; failing too early gives them away.

❓ FAQ

How is The Resistance different from Avalon?

The Resistance has no special roles like Merlin or the Assassin, so it is simpler and great for beginners. Avalon adds role powers and the assassination.
