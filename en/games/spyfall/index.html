Spyfall: moderator & rules guide

Everyone shares a location, except the spy

👥 3–12 players⏳ 10 min⭐ Best 5–8Party games
▶ Start with the app

Want to play Spyfall without anyone sitting out as moderator? Board Game MC narrates it for you in Cantonese, Mandarin or English, with timers and optional digital role dealing. Also known as 間諜危機, 间谍危机, 誰是間諜.

📖 How to play

  1. The phone deals roles: civilians see the same location, the spy only learns they are the spy.
  2. Players take turns asking anyone a question; the answerer asks the next one. Keep questions vague so the spy cannot work out the location.
  3. Anyone can tap "Accuse" to stop the clock. If everyone except the accused agrees, reveal their role.
  4. The spy may reveal themselves at any time and guess the location. A correct guess wins.
  5. If time runs out, each player gets one accusation. If none succeeds, the spy wins.

🧭 Spyfall role strategy

🕶️

Spy

  • Answer vaguely while working out the location from the questions.
  • Reveal and guess as soon as you are confident.
🙂

Civilian

  • Ask questions insiders find easy but the spy cannot use.
  • If you suspect someone, ask something only an insider could answer.

💡 Tips

  • Ask questions insiders can answer easily but that reveal little to the spy.
  • The spy can echo other answers or stay vague.
  • If you suspect someone, ask something only an insider could answer.

❓ FAQ

Do I need the Spyfall cards?

No. The phone deals the location and roles and shows the full location list for the spy.

How long is a round?

8 minutes by default; change it in the options.
