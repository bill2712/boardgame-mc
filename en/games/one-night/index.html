One Night Ultimate Werewolf: moderator & rules guide

One night, swapped cards, ten minutes to find the wolf

👥 3–10 players⏳ 10 min⭐ Best 5–8Social deduction
▶ Start with the app

Want to play One Night Ultimate Werewolf without anyone sitting out as moderator? Board Game MC narrates it for you in Cantonese, Mandarin or English, with timers and optional digital role dealing. Also known as 一夜終極狼人, 一夜狼人, 一夜终极狼人.

📖 How to play

  1. Use player count + 3 cards: one for each player and three in the center.
  2. At night roles wake in order. Cards may get swapped, and your final role is the card in front of you.
  3. At dawn, discuss. When time is up everyone points at once; the player with the most votes (at least 2) dies.
  4. If a Werewolf dies, the village wins. If no Werewolf dies, the werewolves win. If the Tanner dies, the Tanner wins.

💡 Tips

  • Robbers and Troublemakers swap cards, so your final role may not be the one you saw.
  • If everyone claims Villager, the werewolves may be in the center.
  • Werewolves like to claim Troublemaker or Drunk, since those roles never see their new card.
  • The Tanner should look suspicious, but not too obviously.

❓ FAQ

How many cards does ONUW need?

Players plus three: one each and three in the center. The app checks the total for you.

What is the night wake order?

Doppelgänger → Werewolves → Minion → Masons → Seer → Robber → Troublemaker → Drunk → Insomniac.
