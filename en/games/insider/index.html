Insider: moderator & rules guide

Guess the word with yes/no questions, then catch the Insider

👥 4–8 players⏳ 15 min⭐ Best 5–7Word games
▶ Start with the app

Want to play Insider without anyone sitting out as moderator? Board Game MC narrates it for you in Cantonese, Mandarin or English, with timers and optional digital role dealing. Also known as 內鬼, 誰是內鬼, Insider 內鬼.

📖 How to play

  1. The Master (public) and the Insider (secret) know the word. Everyone else is a Common.
  2. Ask the Master yes/no questions. The Master may only answer "Yes", "No" or "I don't know". Guess the word before time runs out.
  3. Once guessed, discuss for as long as the guessing took to find the Insider, then vote together.
  4. Catch the Insider and the Master and Commons win; miss and the Insider wins. No correct guess in time means everyone loses.

💡 Tips

  • The Insider shouldn't solve it too fast; it shortens discussion but looks suspicious.
  • Watch for questions that are suspiciously on target.
  • The Master should answer fairly and favour no one.

❓ FAQ

How long is the discussion after guessing?

As long as the guessing took. The app works it out for you.
