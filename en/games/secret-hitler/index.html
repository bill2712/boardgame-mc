Secret Hitler: moderator & rules guide

1930s Germany: Liberals vs. hidden Fascists

👥 5–10 players⏳ 45 min⭐ Best 7–10Social deduction
▶ Start with the app

Want to play Secret Hitler without anyone sitting out as moderator? Board Game MC narrates it for you in Cantonese, Mandarin or English, with timers and optional digital role dealing. Also known as 秘密希特勒, 秘密希特拉, Secret Dictator.

📖 How to play

  1. Liberals win by enacting 5 Liberal policies or executing Hitler.
  2. Fascists win by enacting 6 Fascist policies, or electing Hitler Chancellor after 3 Fascist policies.
  3. Each round the President nominates a Chancellor and everyone votes. If it passes, the President draws 3 and discards 1, and the Chancellor discards 1 and enacts 1.
  4. Three failed elections in a row enact the top policy of the deck.
  5. The app narrates the night and tracks policies, the election tracker and presidential powers.

🧭 Secret Hitler role strategy

🕊️

Liberal

  • Check whether the President's and Chancellor's stories match.
  • After three Fascist policies, choose Chancellors very carefully.
🦅

Fascist

  • Build trust for Hitler so he can be elected Chancellor.
  • Enacting a Liberal policy or two buys trust.
💀

Hitler

  • Act like the most reliable Liberal; do not go on the attack.
  • With 7+ players you do not know your team; watch who helps you.

💡 Tips

  • After three Fascist policies, choose Chancellors carefully; electing Hitler loses instantly.
  • If the President and Chancellor disagree about the cards, at least one is lying.
  • Hitler should act like the most trustworthy Liberal at the table.
  • Use the Investigate power to confirm your strongest suspicion.

❓ FAQ

What is the night phase in Secret Hitler?

5–6 players: Fascists and Hitler see each other. 7–10 players: Fascists see each other while Hitler keeps eyes closed with a thumb up.

Does the app track presidential powers?

Yes. The policy board shows the power for each Fascist policy at your player count and reads it out.
